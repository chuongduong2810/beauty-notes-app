Additional Feature Requirements — 3D Workspace Experience

The workspace should be designed as a fully immersive 3D room rather than a traditional flat canvas. Users should feel like they are physically inside a creative environment where notes exist naturally within the space.

Key requirements:

* The entire workspace should be a navigable 3D room.
* Users must be able to click and drag the mouse to rotate the camera and freely look around the room.
* Sticky notes should be draggable and attachable to any wall or surface inside the room.
* Notes should behave like real paper notes pinned or taped onto walls, including subtle realistic effects such as shadows, depth, slight bending, and natural movement.
* The interaction should feel tactile and cinematic, not mechanical.

Focus Interaction:

* When a user clicks on a sticky note, the note should smoothly zoom toward the screen and become the primary focus.
* The background environment should softly blur during focus mode to create depth and immersion.
* Users should be able to directly edit the content of the note while in this focused state.
* Exiting focus mode should smoothly transition the user back into the 3D room environment.

The overall experience should feel atmospheric, calming, and visually polished — combining productivity with immersive spatial interaction.
